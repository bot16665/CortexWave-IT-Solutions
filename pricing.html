Frequently Asked Questions

Is the site visit really free with no obligation? +
Yes, completely free. We conduct detailed site assessment at no cost, and you're under no obligation to avail our services. This is our way to understand your requirements better.
Do prices include hardware/parts cost? +
Our quotation includes both hardware and service charges with detailed breakup. We source components from authorized vendors ensuring warranty and quality. All costs are transparent and itemized.
Can I get an itemized quotation? +
Absolutely. Every quotation is itemized showing PC specifications, software licenses, networking components, and service charges separately for complete transparency.
Is there a discount for bulk systems (50+ PCs)? +
Yes! Bulk orders get special pricing. Contact us with your requirements and we'll provide customized quotes for large-scale deployments. The more systems, the better the per-unit cost.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept bank transfers, cheques, and online payments. For large contracts, we can arrange flexible payment schedules aligned with project milestones.
What's the typical delivery timeline? +
For small orders: 3-5 days. For large college lab projects: Depends on scope. We'll provide a detailed project timeline with your quotation.
Do you provide AMC (Annual Maintenance Contract)? +
Yes! AMC options are available for systems we've set up. This includes scheduled maintenance, updates, troubleshooting, and priority on-site support.
What if I need different specifications for different systems? +
No problem! We customize specifications based on your requirements. Different departments can have different PC configurations tailored to their needs.
